Text version of all steps (for printing or screen readers)

Task 1: Post a stamped BeReal

  1. Tap the 12:00 AM alert. Your lock screen. The alert means this month's Community Boards are open. It isn't a timed post, so open it whenever suits you.
  2. Tap Got it. A one-time intro shows how it works: take a photo, choose a Community Board, get a Real Passport stamp.
  3. Tap Opt-in to join Community Boards. The Today screen. Boards stay off until you join, and friends' posts stay blurred until you post. Tip: You only opt in once.
  4. Tap the capture button. You've joined all six October boards. Each tile shows its stamp, lock state and how many friends have posted.
  5. Tap the shutter to take your photo. The familiar BeReal dual camera. You choose a board after the photo, so capturing stays fast.
  6. Tap COMMUNITY BOARD. Add a caption if you like, then pick the post type. Tip: SEND posts a regular BeReal to My Friends, with no stamp.
  7. Tap the CRAMMING board. This month's boards. Pick the one that fits your photo.
  8. Tap Stamp & post to CRAMMING. The CRAMMING card turns white and unlocks, so you know it's selected.
  9. Wait a moment. “Stamping your passport..” shows while your post saves.
  10. Tap See it on the board. “Stamp earned! Added to CRAMMING board 🎉” Your new stamp is in your Passport. Tip: Open my Passport takes you straight to your collection.

Done: Your post sits in the CRAMMING board feed with its passport stamp, next to your squad's posts.

Task 2: Check your Passport

  1. Tap Passport. You're on the board feed. Your Passport tab sits at the top.
  2. Tap View October Capsule Carousel. Your Passport. The squad leaderboard ranks everyone by stamps, and the October stamp gallery shows CRAMMING as Unlocked.
  3. Tap any photo. The October capsule: every post from this month's boards, saved to Memories. Tip: Tap Board details to see who took part.
  4. Tap the arrow for the next photo. The photo opens full size, with who posted it and which board.
  5. Tap X to close. Keep stepping through the carousel with the arrows.

Done: When a month ends, its boards stay here in Memories as capsules.

Task 3: Opt in or out of boards

  1. Tap Profile. Board settings live under your profile.
  2. Tap Opt-in - Community Board. Settings. Community Board options sit under Features. Tip: Opt-out - Community Board opens the same list.
  3. Tap a board to switch it on or off. Here, COFFEE. Each current board has its own ON / OFF switch.

Done: COFFEE is now off, and the app confirms “Left COFFEE.” Turning every board off takes you back to the opt-in screen. Your regular BeReal works the same either way.
