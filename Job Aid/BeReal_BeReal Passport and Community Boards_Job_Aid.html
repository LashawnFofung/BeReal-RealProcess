BeReal Passport & Community Boards Job Aid

A quick guide for app users: join a board, post a stamped BeReal, and manage your boards.

Updated October 27, 2026Prototype

Before you start

Use this guide to join a Community Board, post a stamped BeReal, and manage your boards. It's written for first-time users and testers of the clickable prototype.

  • Try it: lashawnfofung.github.io/BeReal-RealProcess (phone or desktop)
  • You need: the prototype link only. No account or sign-in.
  • Demo panel (desktop): switch between new and returning user, pick a reminder style, or force an error to see every screen.

Key terms

TermWhat it means
Community BoardA themed monthly board for your squad (Cramming, Hike, Coffee, Haunted, Game Time, Little Wins). Opt-in only.
Post typeSEND = a regular BeReal to My Friends, no stamp. COMMUNITY BOARD = a themed post that earns a stamp.
Passport stampThe badge you collect each time you post to a board. New designs launch every month.
BeReal PassportYour tab that holds your stamp collection and the squad leaderboard.
CapsuleA finished month's board, saved to Memories as a carousel or collage.
SquadYour friend group. Reactions stay inside the squad, with no public likes.

1Join a board and post a stamped BeReal

Use this for the core loop: capture first, pick a board, collect a stamp.

StepDo thisYou'll see
1Tap the 12:00 AM alert on your lock screen.The Today screen. It's not a timed post, so open it whenever suits you.
2Tap Opt-in to join Community Boards (first time only).A one-time intro, then this month's boards.
3Tap the capture button and take your photo.The familiar dual-camera shot. Tap the small photo to swap cameras.
4Add a caption, then choose COMMUNITY BOARD.The post-type toggle switches to the themed post.
5Pick a board, such as Cramming.The card turns from grey to white and its lock icon unlocks.
6Post.Stamping your passport.. then Stamp earned! Added to Cramming board 🎉
Note:Choosing SEND at step 4 posts a regular BeReal with no stamp.

2Check your Passport, leaderboard and capsule

Use this to see your stamps, where your squad ranks, and past months. All three live under the Passport tab.

StepDo thisYou'll see
1Tap Passport on the Today screen.This month's stamp gallery. Stamps you've earned show as unlocked.
2Look at the Squad Stamp Tally.The leaderboard, ranked by stamps collected.
3Tap View October Capsule Carousel.Past boards saved in Memories.
4Tap any photo in the capsule.The photo opens; step through the carousel.

3Opt in or out of boards

Use this to join or leave any current board, one board at a time.

StepDo thisYou'll see
1Tap your profile, then Settings.The Settings screen.
2Under Features, tap Opt-in - Community Board or Opt-out - Community Board.The Community Board list.
3Switch each board ON or OFF.The toggle updates for that board.
Note:Turning every board off takes you back to the opt-in screen. Your regular BeReal works the same either way.

Troubleshooting

If this happensTry this
No stamp after postingCheck that you chose COMMUNITY BOARD, not SEND, at step 4.
Boards don't show on TodayYou haven't opted in yet. Tap Opt-in to join Community Boards.
Friends' posts look blurredPost your own BeReal first to unlock the feed.
A board shows a lock iconThat's normal. The lock opens when you pick that board for a post.
A board is missing from SettingsOnly current boards can be changed. Past months live in Memories as capsules.
Too many remindersOpt out of boards you don't use in Settings.
Good to know:Boards reset each month, so there's no streak or stamp to lose.
